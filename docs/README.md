## Project Management at OctoAcme

OctoAcme uses a structured, iterative project management framework grounded in customer-first delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. The process follows five core phases: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective & Continuous Improvement. Every project is led by a named Project Manager (PM), who coordinates delivery and manages risks, and a Product Lead (Product Manager), who defines outcomes and measures success. This role clarity ensures both accountability and a clear separation between strategic vision and execution details.

Project execution emphasizes transparency, predictability, and team rhythm. Key practices include daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, regularly scheduled demos, and sprint-based planning. Work is visualized on project boards and managed with a consistent workflow from Backlog through QA to Done. Pull requests and testing are integral to the quality assurance process, with requirements for automated tests, linting, security scanning, and peer review prior to merge. Manual QA is performed before significant releases, and incident playbooks and checklists help contain and remediate issues swiftly.

Communication is structured both within the delivery team and with stakeholders. Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers collaborate closely: standups, weekly alignments, and monthly stakeholder updates ensure continuous feedback and shared context. Stakeholder communication templates support consistent reporting of progress, risks, and decisions needed. Escalation paths are clearly defined, supporting rapid resolution of blockers and incidents.

Continuous improvement is fundamental. After each sprint, release, or milestone, the team holds retrospectives to capture learnings and turn them into actionable items tracked on the project board. Progress on improvements is reviewed in weekly syncs, with a culture of celebrating wins and favoring small, iterative enhancements to maximize lasting value.
